1. Feasibility Check Flowchart

Marketing raises request ➔ Sampling team responds (Yes / No / Maybe) ➔ Marketing accepts or rejects ➔ If accepted, unlocks Mockup, Sampling, or Costing

Marketing ➔ Sampling Team
Exact Feasibility Workflow:
1. Marketing raises a Feasibility Check request ➔ Directly sent to Sampling Team.
2. Sampling Team reviews packaging specs & responds with: YES (Feasible), NO (Not Feasible), or MAYBE (Conditional).
3. Sampling Team's response returns to Marketing.
4. Marketing Decision: • If Marketing chooses Reject (or Sampling said No) ➔ Request is closed.
• If Sampling said Yes or Maybe AND Marketing chooses Accept ➔ Marketing unlocks options in the system to raise: Mockup (Studio), Sampling (Physical Sample), or Costing (Price/BOM)!

2. Sample Request Flowchart (4 Options & Plant Khaniwade)

Draft ➔ Released ➔ Dispatched to matching teams ➔ Client Approves ➔ Khaniwade Plant (1505) produces

Draft ➔ Release ➔ Khaniwade 1505
Important Routing & Plant Rule:
• If a client only wants Design, Mockup, or Costing, the request finishes at that team. Plant is skipped.
• Khaniwade Plant (1505) ONLY gets involved when a physical sample / mass production is confirmed by the client!

3. Seasonal Program Planning Flowchart

Dual Sign-Off: Sampling Team validates materials + Khaniwade Plant (1505) validates machine capacity

Sampling + Plant (1505 Khaniwade)
Dual Sign-Off System:
Both Sampling Team (for paper quality) and Khaniwade Plant (1505) (for factory capacity) must sign off before mass production can begin.
